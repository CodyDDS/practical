The Decline

Women in STEM education: starting vs finishing. Each grid has 100 dots, so every dot stands for 1 in every 100 students (1%).

The left grid shows enrollment: out of every 100 students starting a STEM course, the purple dots are women and the grey dots are men. The right grid shows the female graduation rate: out of every 100, the purple dots are the women who graduate.

The purple circles grow and shrink with each percentage, and the beat drops lower as the share of women falls. Click to play...
